# Email Marketing Strategy — Dull is Dead / DiD

## 01 — Role of Email for DiD

Email is not DiD’s main acquisition channel. It is DiD’s controlled relationship channel.

For a selective B2B agency, email must do four things:

1. Build trust with ambitious brands before they apply.
2. Nurture brands repérées manuellement by DiD.
3. Convert qualified prospects into candidatures or discovery calls.
4. Keep selected brands, partners and warm prospects connected to the DiD worldview.

The tone must be direct, sharp and useful. DiD should not sound like a newsletter d’agence classique. Every email should feel like a diagnostic note from a strategist who sees the gap between a brand’s potential and its current expression.

---

## 02 — Primary Email Goals

**Generate qualified applications**

The main commercial goal is not volume. It is qualified brand applications from ambitious companies with traction, budget authority and willingness to be challenged.

**Nurture manually scouted brands**

DiD will identify brands by hand. Email must help turn a cold observation into a credible, personalized invitation to apply.

**Build authority around anti-banality**

DiD must own the idea that banality is a business risk, not an aesthetic preference.

**Support the Cheapmium model**

Email should explain the fixed fee + growth upside model clearly, without making it sound cheap or risky.

**Create a warm pipeline**

Before a brand applies, DiD should already have shared enough thinking to make the application feel like the next logical step.

## 03 — Audience Segments

| Segment | Description | Email approach |
|---|---|---|
| Newsletter subscribers | Warm audience interested in brand strategy, anti-banality and growth | Educational, diagnostic, manifesto-driven |
| Candidature leads | Brands that started or completed an application | Clear process, selection criteria, next steps |
| Manually scouted brands | Brands identified by DiD but not yet engaged | Personalized, specific, non-generic outreach |
| Qualified prospects | Brands that match selection criteria but have not applied | Strategic nurture, case-based, invitation-led |

| Partners / referrers | Accountants, lawyers, founders, investors, local ecosystem actors | Short updates, selective opportunities, referral framing |
| Unengaged subscribers | No meaningful engagement in 90 days | Re-engagement, then suppression |

## 04 — List Building Strategy

DiD should not build a large, low-quality list. The list must be narrow, qualified and strategically aligned.

### Primary lead magnets

**Anti-Banality Audit**

A short diagnostic framework for brands to score their own banality.

Sections:
- Positioning
- Messaging
- Visual identity
- Website
- Content
- Acquisition
- Measurement

CTA:
> Votre marque est-elle mémorable ou interchangeable ?

**Guide: La banalité coûte plus cher qu’un repositionnement**

A concise PDF explaining how generic communication reduces memorability, preference, conversion and perceived value.

CTA:
> Recevoir le diagnostic anti-banalité.

**Checklist: Votre marque peut-elle postuler chez DiD ?**

A self-selection checklist based on ambition, traction, product quality, refusal of banality, execution capacity and fit humain.

CTA:
> Vérifier si votre marque peut postuler.

**Newsletter: Potentiel / Banalité**

A bi-weekly letter with sharp observations, before/after message rewrites, brand autopsies and strategic notes.

CTA:
> Recevoir les notes DiD.

### Opt-in placements

- Website hero secondary CTA: `Recevoir l’Anti-Banality Audit`
- Website footer CTA: `Votre marque peut postuler`
- LinkedIn profile link
- LinkedIn posts with diagnostic threads
- Case study pages
- Manifesto page
- Candidature confirmation page
- Partner/referral pages

## 06 — Email Types

### 1. Newsletter / brand letter

**Frequency:** Twice per month.

**Purpose:** Build authority and keep DiD top of mind.

**Content mix:**
- 60% diagnostic thinking
- 20% examples / before-after
- 20% DiD point of view, selection, model, case notes

**Example themes:**
- Pourquoi votre marque vend peut-être déjà sans marquer.
- La banalité n’est pas un style. C’est une fuite de valeur.
- 5 signes que votre site cache votre potentiel.
- Votre marque n’a pas un problème de visibilité. Elle a un problème de différence.
- Le fixe est bas. L’exigence ne l’est pas.

### 2. Educational campaigns

**Frequency:** 1–2 per month.

**Purpose:** Convert subscribers into qualified applications.

**Examples:**
- Anti-Banality Audit launch.
- Guide: La banalité coûte plus cher qu’un repositionnement.
- Checklist: Votre marque peut-elle postuler ?
- Mini masterclass: construire une baseline avant une campagne.

### 3. Scouting emails

**Frequency:** As needed, personalized.

**Purpose:** Turn manually identified brands into candidatures.

**Rules:**
- Always start with a specific observation.
- Never use generic praise.
- Never say “nous aimerions collaborer” without diagnosis.
- Mention one visible gap between product potential and brand expression.
- End with a clear invitation to apply.

### 4. Application flow

**Trigger:** Brand starts or submits candidature.

**Purpose:** Clarify the process and reinforce selectivity.

**Emails:**
1. Confirmation.
2. What happens next.
3. Selection criteria reminder.
4. Accepted / refused / needs more time.

### 5. Refusal flow

**Purpose:** Stay firm without burning bridges.

**Tone:** Respectful, clear, non-negotiable.

**Do not:**
- Over-explain.
- Insult the brand.
- Promise future work.
- Soften the decision into ambiguity.

### 6. Partner/referral emails

**Purpose:** Activate the local and professional ecosystem.

**Audience:**
- Founders.
- Investors.
- Lawyers.
- Accountants.
- Business mentors.
- Local incubators.
- Former corporate contacts.

**Message:**
DiD is looking for ambitious brands with real potential, not generic communication projects.

## 09 — Email Design System

DiD emails should feel like strategic notes, not agency newsletters.

### Design rules

- Single column.
- High contrast: DiD Black, Bone White, DiD Red.
- Short paragraphs.
- One primary CTA.
- Minimal images.
- Plain-text option for founder-led emails.
- No stock imagery.
- No decorative icons.
- Use red only for emphasis, CTA or warning-like labels.

### Email components

| Component | Rule |
|---|---|
| Sender name | `DiD` or `Camille / Manon / Véronique / Julien` for personalized notes |
| Subject line | 35–50 characters, sharp, no clickbait |
| Preview text | Extends the subject with a diagnostic angle |
| Header | Simple logo or text signature |
| Body | Short, direct, useful |
| CTA | One primary action |
| Footer | Legal address, unsubscribe, RGPD consent note |

## 10 — Deliverability Rules

DiD must protect deliverability from day one.

### Technical setup

- Custom sending domain: `hello@dullisdead.fr` or similar.
- SPF configured.
- DKIM configured.
- DMARC configured.
- No sending from free Gmail address at scale.
- No purchased lists.
- No imported cold lists without consent.
- Warm up domain gradually.

### List hygiene

- Double opt-in for newsletter where possible.
- Remove hard bounces immediately.
- Suppress unsubscribes permanently.
- Review unengaged subscribers every 90 days.
- Keep cold outreach highly personalized and low volume.

### Cold outreach caution

For manually scouted brands, DiD can send personalized B2B emails, but must remain compliant with RGPD:

- Legitimate interest must be documented.
- Messages must be relevant to the recipient’s professional role.
- Unsubscribe/opt-out must be clear.
- Do not send mass generic cold campaigns.

### Application confirmation

**Subject:** `Candidature reçue. Nous lisons les marques, pas les formulaires.`

Bonjour [Prénom],

Votre candidature a bien été reçue.

Chez DiD, nous ne traitons pas les candidatures comme des leads. Nous lisons les marques : ambition, traction, produit, expression actuelle, capacité d’exécution et fit humain.

- retenue pour un échange,
- besoin d’informations complémentaires,
- non retenue pour ce cycle.

Nous préférons être clairs plutôt que disponibles.

— DiD

### Refusal email

**Subject:** `Votre marque n’est pas retenue pour ce cycle`

Bonjour [Prénom],

Merci pour votre candidature.

Après analyse, nous ne retenons pas [Marque] pour ce cycle. Le potentiel n’est pas encore assez clair au regard de nos critères actuels : ambition, traction, capacité d’exécution, refus de la banalité et alignement économique.

Cela ne signifie pas que votre marque ne peut pas grandir. Cela signifie simplement que DiD n’est pas le bon partenaire à ce stade.

Nous vous souhaitons une excellente continuation.

— DiD

## 13 — Strategic Recommendation

DiD should use email as a selective nurture system, not a mass newsletter.

The best email strategy for DiD is:

1. Build a small, high-quality list around the Anti-Banality Audit.
2. Send sharp diagnostic content twice per month.
3. Use personalized scouting emails for manually identified brands.
4. Turn the candidature process into an automated but human-feeling flow.
5. Measure applications, calls and selected projects, not just opens and clicks.

Email should make one thing obvious:

**DiD does not chase brands. DiD chooses brands worth growing.**
